# Methods and references
- **Nearest-neighbor Tm**: SantaLucia J Jr (1998) PNAS 95:1460-1465. Na+ correction: dS += 0.368 * (N-1) * ln[Na+]. Mg2+ is not modeled.
- **Wallace rule**: Tm = 2(A+T) + 4(G+C), for short oligos.
- **Standard curve efficiency**: E = 10^(-1/slope) - 1. Acceptable 90-110%, R^2 >= 0.98 (MIQE, Bustin et al. 2009).
- **Livak 2^-ddCq**: Livak & Schmittgen (2001) Methods 25:402-408.
- **Pfaffl ratio**: Pfaffl (2001) Nucleic Acids Res 29:e45.
- **Purity ratios**: A260/A280 ~1.8 (DNA), ~2.0 (RNA); A260/A230 ~2.0-2.2. Ratios are indicative, not proof of purity.
- **Dosing**: arithmetic only; always verify against label/formulary and veterinarian.
